  A([Brand context agent]):::agent --> W[Write cited ad briefs]:::agent
  A --> F[Pick formats per product]:::code
  C([Competitor ads, live 30+ days]):::in --> F
  F --> W
  W --> G[Compliance gate: rules + judge]:::code
  G -- flagged --> R[Rewrite flagged lines, max 3]:::agent
  R --> G
  G -- passed --> S[Fit Minimalist's text budget]:::agent
  O([Minimalist's own top ads]):::in --> S
  S --> T[Hindi and Tamil versions]:::agent
  B([Asset library agent]):::agent --> P[Product image, label-checked]:::img
  S --> P
  P --> Q[Scene images, in parallel]:::img
  P --> M[Compose ads, three sizes]:::code
  Q --> M
  T --> M
  M --> K[Score, risk and warnings]:::code
  K --> L[(Ad library: 433 ads)]:::code
  K --> H[Reviewer signs every line]:::human
  H --> D([Download with review ticket]):::human
